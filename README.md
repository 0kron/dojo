# Dojo 
A place to save, manage and share my notes, academic writings and exercises. The purpose is to actually be able to reference myself as I keep learning new things in the fields of Mathematics, Computer Science and Programming. 

## About the folders
- **Competitive Programming**:
    - Focuses on solved problems, with specific directories for [CSES](https://cses.fi/problemset/list), and [CodeForces](https://codeforces.com/problemset).
    - As usual the implementations are in C++17.
- **Mathematics**: 
    - Series of PDFs of a specific subject/university course I took or am taking, this notes would depend on my source of information for its language, either Spanish or English.
    - Typst source code for all templates, and documents may be also found in this directory.
- **Computer Science**:
    - Depending on the directory/source there would be a PDF of my notes or a series of excersizes I solved.
- **Thesis**:
    - The messiest directory, a place of resources, findings, excersizes and drafts directed to write my undergrad thesis.

> Each of the three directories has its own `README.md` as Index to quickly scheme through its content.
